## B. App Review Information →「Notes」（英文，直接貼）

```
This is a bug-fix release. Version 1.4.9 (build 75) shipped with a regression: a bad
merge dropped several features that were present and working in 1.4.8 (build 68).
Users who updated lost the in-app help sections for the two widgets, the entry point
that tells free users the Lock Screen countdown exists, the satellite high-resolution
notice, and continuous foreground location updates. The 1.4.9 release notes on the
store describe some of these features, so the shipped app does not match its own
description. This build restores all of them and adds no new capabilities.

Also fixed in this build:
- Map layers: after a pinch-zoom the rail lines briefly snapped back to their old
  position before settling; while panning, the rail lines and trains lagged behind
  the base map by roughly one frame. Both are corrected.
- Taiwan Railway station board: you can now pick a single train to track. Its
  scheduled time and the operator's official delay figure are shown on the Lock
  Screen (Live Activity).
- Android-only rating link had been flattened to an iOS App Store URL. Not visible
  on iOS; corrected for completeness.

Permissions: unchanged from 1.4.8/1.4.9. Location is optional and only used to centre
the map and list nearby stations; nothing is uploaded. The app works fully without it.

Paid features: the app is free. An optional subscription ("軌島通行證" / Rail Island
Pass) unlocks extras such as multi-station widgets and the Lock Screen countdown.
No account or login is required to review the app; every core feature — live train
animation, station boards, timetables, route search — is available without paying
and without signing in.

Data source: all train data comes from Taiwan's official open transport API (TDX,
Ministry of Transportation and Communications) and the operators' own public feeds.

Expedited review requested because the currently live version is missing features it
advertises, and users have reported the missing functionality.
```
