## 這一顆載了什麼

### 新功能：台鐵等站卡

台鐵車站看板的標題列多一顆「追蹤一班車」，點了列出接下來最多 8 班（表定時刻／車種車次／
往終點／官方誤點），選一班就在鎖定畫面開一張卡。

卡片主角是**「實際約到站」的鐘面時刻**（表定 ＋ 官方誤點分鐘），例如
「表定 18:32・誤點 3 分（實際約 18:35）」。官方沒給誤點時只印表定、並明說「目前無即時誤點資訊」。

🔴 **刻意不做秒級倒數**：台鐵官方公開資料只有「表訂時刻」與「誤點分鐘」兩個值，
沒有預估到站時刻。把分鐘換算成秒級倒數等於製造官方沒有的精度。

## Review Notes（審查備註・英文）

```
This update adds a Live Activity for Taiwan Railway station boards. The user picks a
specific train from the station board; the lock-screen card shows that train's scheduled
departure time and the official delay in minutes, published by the Taiwan Railway
Administration through the government open-data platform (TDX).

The card deliberately shows a wall-clock time rather than a live countdown: the official
feed publishes only a scheduled time and a delay in minutes, so a second-level countdown
would present precision the source does not provide.

Location is used only while the app is in the foreground, unchanged from previous versions.
No new permissions are requested.
```
