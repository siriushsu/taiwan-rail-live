Taichung MRT Green Line: Map, Stations & Operating Hours

Taichung MRT Green Line (Wuriwenxin Beitun Line): 18 stations from Beitun Main to HSR Taichung; 4 stations connect to other lines, Taiwan Railway (TRA) or High Speed Rail. Rail Island's data sources have no train-by-train timetable for this line, so only the operating hours and headways are listed below.
