Taipei MRT Songshan-Xindian Line: Map, Stations & First/Last Trains

Taipei MRT Songshan-Xindian Line: 20 stations from Xindian to Songshan, plus the Xiaobitan Branch Line (Qizhang – Xiaobitan); 8 stations connect to other lines, Taiwan Railway (TRA) or High Speed Rail. Below are the first and last trains in both directions, headways and the transfers at each station.

"First" and "last" are the earliest and latest departures from the starting terminus of trains that run all the way to the other end; trains that stop short and leave earlier or later than these are listed separately. Other day types and every station are further down.
