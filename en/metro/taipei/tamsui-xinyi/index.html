Taipei MRT Tamsui-Xinyi Line: Map, Stations & First/Last Trains

Taipei MRT Tamsui-Xinyi Line: 29 stations from Guangci/Fengtian Temple to Tamsui, plus the Xinbeitou Branch Line (Beitou – Xinbeitou); 7 stations connect to other lines, Taiwan Railway (TRA) or High Speed Rail. Below are the first and last trains in both directions, headways and the transfers at each station.
