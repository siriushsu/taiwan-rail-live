Banqiao Station Timetable: TRA and HSR Train Times and Transfers

Banqiao Station connects TRA, HSR, the Taipei MRT Bannan Line and the New Taipei Metro Circular Line, making it a multi-system transfer station in New Taipei City. Rail Island groups same-name stations of different systems into one transfer hint. This page lists train-by-train times and running days for TRA Sep 27–Oct 10 and HSR Sep 30–Oct 13, updated once a week.

Transfers and how the stations relate

In the data, the TRA and HSR stations are about 14 m apart, and the Bannan Line and the Circular Line are also within walking-transfer range. Each system still has its own platforms, timetable and live information.

Distances in the data are only used to recognise shared stations and walking transfers. They are not in-station navigation and do not equal the actual walking time between platforms.

How Rail Island shows this station

TRA trains run from the official timetable, and when official real-time delay data is available it is used to correct their position on the timeline. HSR trains run from the official timetable; this page does not claim per-train GPS for high speed rail. Metro trains are shown from the official timetables, headways, arrival countdowns or train movements each operator makes available; how live each system is differs.

The TRA and HSR timetables on this page are updated once a week and list the scheduled trains within the period the data covers. For current trains, delays, suspensions and operator announcements, use the live map, and rely on the operator's information at the station.

For metro lines, first and last trains and headways, see the metro maps.
