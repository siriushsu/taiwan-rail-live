Hsinchu HSR Station Timetable: TRA and HSR Train Times and Transfers

Hsinchu HSR Station is a walking transfer to TRA Liujia Station on the Liujia Line. Rail Island treats HSR Hsinchu and TRA Liujia as one transfer group but keeps the two actual station names. This page lists train-by-train times and running days for TRA Sep 27–Oct 10 and HSR Sep 30–Oct 13, updated once a week.

Transfers and how the stations relate

In the data, Hsinchu HSR Station and TRA Liujia Station are about 122 m apart. They are different places from TRA Hsinchu Station in downtown Hsinchu.

Distances in the data are only used to recognise shared stations and walking transfers. They are not in-station navigation and do not equal the actual walking time between platforms.

How Rail Island shows this station

TRA trains run from the official timetable, and when official real-time delay data is available it is used to correct their position on the timeline. HSR trains run from the official timetable; this page does not claim per-train GPS for high speed rail.

The TRA and HSR timetables on this page are updated once a week and list the scheduled trains within the period the data covers. For current trains, delays, suspensions and operator announcements, use the live map, and rely on the operator's information at the station.
