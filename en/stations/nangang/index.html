Nangang Station Timetable: TRA and HSR Times and Transfers

Nangang Station is a shared station area for TRA, HSR and the Taipei MRT Bannan Line. Rail Island shows all three systems, and does not confuse Nangang with the next station, Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center. This page lists train-by-train times and running days for TRA Sep 27–Oct 10 and HSR Sep 30–Oct 13, updated once a week.

Transfers and how the stations relate

The TRA and HSR stations are about 25 m apart, and Bannan Line Nangang Station is in the same transfer area. Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center is a different metro station, so follow the signs inside the station.

Distances in the data are only used to recognise shared stations and walking transfers. They are not in-station navigation and do not equal the actual walking time between platforms.

How Rail Island shows this station

TRA trains run from the official timetable, and when official real-time delay data is available it is used to correct their position on the timeline. HSR trains run from the official timetable; this page does not claim per-train GPS for high speed rail. Metro trains are shown from the official timetables, headways, arrival countdowns or train movements each operator makes available; the level of real-time data varies by system.

The TRA and HSR timetables on this page are updated once a week and list the scheduled trains within the period the data covers. For current trains, delays, suspensions and operator announcements, use the live map, and rely on the operator's information at the station.
