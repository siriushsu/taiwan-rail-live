Taoyuan HSR Station Timetable: HSR Train Times and Transfers

Taoyuan HSR Station is in Cingpu, Taoyuan, and connects to Taoyuan Airport MRT station A18 Taoyuan HSR Station. Rail Island shows HSR and the Airport MRT separately, then marks them as a transfer. This page lists train-by-train times and running days for HSR Sep 30–Oct 13, updated once a week.

Transfers and how the stations relate

In the data, the HSR station and the Airport MRT station are about 119 m apart. This station is not the same as TRA Taoyuan Station in downtown Taoyuan.

Distances in the data are only used to recognise shared stations and walking transfers. They are not in-station navigation and do not equal the actual walking time between platforms.

How Rail Island shows this station

HSR trains run from the official timetable; this page does not claim per-train GPS for high speed rail. Metro trains are shown from the official timetables, headways, arrival countdowns or train movements each operator makes available; how live each system is differs.

The HSR timetables on this page are updated once a week and list the scheduled trains within the period the data covers. For current trains, delays, suspensions and operator announcements, use the live map, and rely on the operator's information at the station.

For metro lines, first and last trains and headways, see the metro maps.
